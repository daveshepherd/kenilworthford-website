Kenilworth ford is where Finham Brook crosses the B4103 in Kenilworth.

This is guidance based on observations correlated to the level of the brook, use your own judgement before travelling through the ford and if you are unsure use an alternative router.

History levels are available on the Government food warning information site.

This uses Environment Agency flood and river level data from the real-time data API (Beta)
